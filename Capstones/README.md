## 💡 How to Build and Master Capstones
1. **Choose a Project**: Navigate to the level matching your current stage (e.g. [`Capstones/Level_1_Beginner/Capstones.md`](Level_1_Beginner/Capstones.md)).
2. **Review the Real-World Scenario & Requirements**: Study the problem domain and architectural constraints.
3. **Write Your Solution**: Implement the project from scratch in Python.
4. **Compare Output & Architecture**: Compare your console output against the **Expected Output** and expand the **Solution & Explanation** accordion to analyze design patterns, performance characteristics, and best practices.
